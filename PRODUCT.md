# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Job seekers and early-career professionals comparing a PDF resume with a specific job description before applying. They need fast evidence about fit, gaps, and interview preparation without recruiter-style harshness.

## Product Purpose

Turn an uploaded resume and pasted job description into a validated, explainable match report. Success means the user can identify credible strengths, missing evidence, and concrete next actions in one review session.

## Brand Personality

Credible, calm, practical.

## Anti-references

Avoid recruiter rejection dashboards, generic AI SaaS landing pages, oversized score theatrics, decorative gradients, glass effects, nested card grids, and unsupported certainty.

## Design Principles

- Evidence before judgment.
- Show the next useful action, not generic encouragement.
- Keep upload, validation, and provider state explicit.
- Make the report scannable without hiding nuance.
- Treat sample and mock output honestly.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support keyboard use, visible focus, reduced motion, screen-reader status updates, sufficient contrast, non-color state cues, and touch targets of at least 44 pixels.
